# Cookie policy

{site.name} uses a few cookies and similar technologies (local storage). This page lists them and explains how to control them.

## Strictly necessary

These make the service work. They need no consent.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `better-auth.session_token` | Keeps you signed in | 7 days |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembers the language you chose | 1 year |
| `theme` (local storage) | Remembers light or dark mode | Until you clear it |

## Measurement

We measure how the service is used with PostHog.

{/* #region turbinekit:consent */}

Before you choose, and if you refuse, PostHog keeps no cookie and does not identify you. If you accept, it sets this cookie:

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ph_*_posthog` | Counts visits and connects them to your account | 1 year |
