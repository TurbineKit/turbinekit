# Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data {site.company.name} ("we") collects when you use {site.name}, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## Who we are

{site.company.name} operates {site.name}. For any question about your data, write to <a href={`mailto:${site.company.email}`}>{site.company.email}</a>.

## What we collect

- **Account data**: your name, email address and a hash of your password, or the profile your sign-in provider (Google or GitHub) shares with us.
- **Security data**: your passkeys, your two-factor settings, your active sessions with their IP address and browser, and the API keys you create.
- **Connected apps**: the AI assistants you allow to act on your account, and what each may do.
- **Content**: the data you create in the app.
- **Files**: the images you upload, such as your avatar. {/* turbinekit:files */}
- **Billing data**: your plan and payment history. Stripe processes your card; we never see the full card number. {/* turbinekit:payments */}
- **Usage data**: the pages you view and the features you use, measured with PostHog. {/* turbinekit:analytics */}

## Why we use it

- To provide the service and keep your account secure.
- To send the emails you need: email verification, password resets and security notices.
- To prevent abuse, for example with rate limits on sign-in attempts.
- To comply with the law.
- To understand how the service is used and improve it, only with your consent where the law requires it. {/* turbinekit:analytics */}

## Who receives it

We share personal data only with providers that process it on our behalf: hosting, database, email delivery, and the services named above. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to some uses of it. Most of this is available in your account settings. For anything else, write to <a href={`mailto:${site.company.email}`}>{site.company.email}</a>. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Cookies

See our [cookie policy](/legal/cookies).

## Changes

We will update the date at the bottom of this page when this policy changes, and tell you by email about any important change.
